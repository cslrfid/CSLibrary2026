Notification and battery

The battery level, the hardware keys and the other reader notifications.

The battery level

The reader reports the battery voltage through the notification subsystem.

reader.notification.OnVoltageEvent += (sender, e) =>
{
    Console.WriteLine($"{e.Voltage} mV");
};

The current level can also be read directly.

uint level = reader.GetCurrentBatteryLevel();

The battery subsystem controls how often the reader reports its level.

reader.battery.SetPollingTime(30);   // every 30 seconds

The hardware keys

The trigger and the keys of the reader are delivered through the key event.

reader.notification.OnKeyEvent += (sender, e) =>
{
    if (e.KeyCode == Key.KEY1 && e.KeyDown)
        Console.WriteLine("The trigger was pressed.");
};

The trigger

The reader can be told to abort the running RFID operation when the trigger is released.

reader.rfid.SetTriggerReleaseAbortRFID(true);

The reader information

The Bluetooth controller and the reader hardware are reached through two more subsystems.

reader.bluetoothIC.GetDeviceName();
reader.bluetoothIC.GetFirmwareVersion();

reader.siliconlabIC.GetFirmwareVersion();
reader.siliconlabIC.GetPCBVersion();

See Notification, battery and hardware in the API reference.
